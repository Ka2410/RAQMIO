How we work

A clear path from need to product.

Every engagement follows the same five steps. The depth of each step depends on the project — the order does not.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Trace the need to the people, decisions and moments it touches.

  2. 02

    Frame

    Define what matters, what is in scope and what the work should change.

  3. 03

    Shape

    Test the structure and interaction before committing to build.

  4. 04

    Make

    Design and engineer a coherent product around the agreed need.

  5. 05

    Review

    Check the result against the goal and agree what comes next.

Nothing is built before the goal and scope are agreed.
